## Pythagorean Identities

Every shaded triangle is one identity.

<Sheet>
<PythagoreanHexagon />

1. $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$
2. $\tan^2\theta + 1 = \sec^2\theta$
3. $1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta$

</Sheet>

## Reciprocal and Quotient

Opposites multiply to 1. Each corner is the product of its neighbours.

<Sheet>
<RecipHexagon />

1. $\sin\theta\,\csc\theta = 1$
2. $\cos\theta\,\sec\theta = 1$
3. $\tan\theta\,\cot\theta = 1$
4. $\tan\theta = \dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$
5. $\cot\theta = \dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$
6. $\sin\theta = \tan\theta\,\cos\theta$

</Sheet>

## The Unit Circle

Six lengths, three similar triangles.

<Sheet>
<UnitCircle />

1. $\cos\theta : \sin\theta : 1$
2. $1 : \tan\theta : \sec\theta$
3. $\cot\theta : 1 : \csc\theta$

</Sheet>

## Special Values

Count to four under a root, then halve.

<Sheet>
<SpecialValues />

1. $\sin\theta = \tfrac{\sqrt{n}}{2},\ n = 0,1,2,3,4$
2. $\cos\theta = \sin(90^\circ - \theta)$
3. $\tan\theta = \dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$

</Sheet>

## Symmetry

Mirror or turn the point; read the new coordinates.

<Sheet>
<Symmetry />

1. $\sin(\pi - \theta) = \sin\theta$
2. $\cos(-\theta) = \cos\theta$
3. $\tan(\pi + \theta) = \tan\theta$
4. $\sin(\tfrac{\pi}{2} - \theta) = \cos\theta$
5. $\sin(\theta \pm \tfrac{\pi}{2}) = \pm\cos\theta$
6. $\cos(\theta \pm \tfrac{\pi}{2}) = \mp\sin\theta$

</Sheet>

## Angle Sum and Difference

Each side of the rectangle is a product of one function of α and one of β.

<Sheet stack>
<AngleSum />

1. $\sin(\alpha + \beta) = \sin\alpha\cos\beta + \cos\alpha\sin\beta$
2. $\cos(\alpha + \beta) = \cos\alpha\cos\beta - \sin\alpha\sin\beta$
3. $\sin(\alpha - \beta) = \sin\alpha\cos\beta - \cos\alpha\sin\beta$
4. $\cos(\alpha - \beta) = \cos\alpha\cos\beta + \sin\alpha\sin\beta$

</Sheet>

## Double Angle

The angle at the rim is half the angle at the centre.

<Sheet>
<DoubleAngle />

1. $\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$
2. $\cos 2\theta = \cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta$
3. $\cos 2\theta = 2\cos^2\theta - 1$
4. $\cos 2\theta = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta$

</Sheet>

## Power Reduction

A square is half a double-frequency wave, lifted by one half.

<Sheet>
<PowerReduction />

1. $\cos^2\theta = \tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{1}{2}\cos 2\theta$
2. $\sin^2\theta = \tfrac{1}{2} - \tfrac{1}{2}\cos 2\theta$

</Sheet>
