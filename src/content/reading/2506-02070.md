## Why it is kept here

Most introductions to generative modeling split cleanly in two: either intuitive blog posts that skip the stochastic calculus, or research papers that assume the continuity equation and score identities are already muscle memory.

Holderrieth and Erives build the full bridge from first principles across seven chapters and five appendices: ODEs and SDEs, conditional versus marginal probability paths, flow matching and score matching equivalence, classifier-free guidance, DiT and latent-space architectures, and discrete diffusion via continuous-time Markov chains.

The archived HTML edition preserves every equation in native MathML alongside the original figures and proofs, restyled for dark-screen reading without altering a word of the source.
